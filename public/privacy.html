Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026

Who We Are

TBF Entertainment is a culture-driven entertainment company operating in publishing, artistry, and media. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information submitted through tbfentertainment.art and our text-message program.

Information We Collect

We collect only what you give us and what we need to operate the site:

  • Name, email address, and mobile phone number you submit through our forms.
  • Inquiry details and messages you choose to send us.
  • Text-message consent records, including the exact consent wording you agreed to, the date and time, the page URL, and, where available, your IP address and browser user agent.
  • Standard technical information needed to operate and protect the website.

How We Use Information

We use information to answer inquiries, deliver requested updates, maintain business and consent records, improve the website, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

Mobile information and SMS consent data are not sold.

Mobile information and opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except the service providers strictly necessary to deliver and support the messaging service (for example, our messaging carrier and our consent-record storage provider). Those providers may process the information only to provide that service to us.

Our Text Message Program

If you opt in, TBF Entertainment sends recurring book-release, event, informational, and promotional text messages from (513) 866-3832.

  • Frequency: up to 4 messages per month.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase.
  • Opt out: reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe.
  • Help: reply HELP to any message, email info@tbfentertainment.art, or call (513) 866-3832.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Full program details are on our SMS Updates page.

Service Providers

We may use service providers for hosting, forms, email delivery, text-message delivery, analytics, security, and record storage. They may process information only as needed to provide those services to us.

Your Choices

You may reply STOP to stop text messages, use an unsubscribe link in an email, or contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, subject to legal and recordkeeping requirements. We retain SMS consent records for as long as required to evidence your opt-in, and we preserve opt-out history so an unsubscribed number is not re-added.

Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No internet system is completely secure. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here.

Children

This website and our text-message program are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information or SMS consent from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
