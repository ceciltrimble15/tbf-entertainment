Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information TBF Entertainment collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective July 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use this website or submit a form, TBF Entertainment may collect information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, telephone number, city, inquiry details, Street Team information, publishing or media requests, and email or SMS consent choices.

We may also collect limited technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the website, such as browser type, device type, and basic server logs.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to inquiries, provide requested information, manage book releases and author campaigns, operate Street Team activities, process publishing, rights, media, or partnership requests, send communications you requested, maintain consent and opt-out records, protect the website, and improve TBF services.

3. Email and SMS Communications

TBF Entertainment sends promotional or informational email and text messages only when a person has requested or consented to receive them. Consent to receive marketing messages is not a condition of purchasing a book or submitting a general inquiry.

TBF Entertainment does not sell mobile telephone information. Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in records, and consent information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

  • SMS frequency: up to 4 messages per month.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe.
  • Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Opt-out requests are honored and retained as suppression records so a number is not automatically re-added.

4. Service Providers

TBF may use service providers to host the website, store submissions, deliver approved communications, provide analytics, or secure the system. These providers receive only the information reasonably needed to perform their services and are not authorized to use SMS opt-in information for unrelated marketing.

5. Amazon and Other Third-Party Links

Book-purchase links may direct you to Amazon or another third-party service. Transactions completed through Amazon are governed by Amazon's own terms, privacy practices, payment processing, fulfillment, shipping, and return policies.

6. Cookies and Technical Data

The website may use essential browser storage, hosting logs, or analytics tools that are necessary to operate, secure, and understand website use. TBF will update this policy if materially different tracking or advertising technologies are added.

7. Data Retention

TBF retains information only as reasonably necessary for business operations, responding to requests, documenting consent, maintaining opt-out suppression, protecting the system, and satisfying legal or recordkeeping obligations.

8. Security

TBF uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Choices

You may request correction or deletion of appropriate records, unsubscribe from email using the provided link, reply STOP to SMS messages, or ask what information TBF maintains about you by emailing info@tbfentertainment.art.

10. Children

This public TBF Entertainment marketing website is not directed to children under 13, and TBF does not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13 through these public forms.

TBF may update this policy as the website or services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published.
