## 0. The two things that gate everything

1. **KDP Select conflict (eBook).** If the Kindle eBook is ever enrolled in **KDP Select**, it becomes Amazon-exclusive for 90 days and **every** wide eBook channel (Draft2Digital, Apple, Kobo, Google Play, B&N Nook, library eBook) is **blocked**. → The eBook must be published **non-exclusive**. _Do not enroll in KDP Select._
2. **ISBN ownership (print).** IngramSpark (and wide print generally) **requires an owned ISBN**. A **KDP-free** ISBN is locked to Amazon and **cannot** be used on IngramSpark. → We must confirm whether **979-8-9967275-0-6 is an owned Bowker ISBN** or a KDP-assigned one before any wide print.

Everything below flows from these two.
